CampusFlow AI Synthetic Datathon Dataset
Folder: 02_education_campus_space
Files:
- rooms.csv: Campus room inventory, attributes, facilities, and accessibility
- class_schedule.csv: Recurring departmental lecture and lab schedules by weekday
- room_requests.csv: Historical student and faculty room request demand (demand data without room allocations)
- events.csv: One-off campus events with expected vs actual attendance

Data Limitation Disclosure:
This dataset is synthetic campus-space data generated for university datathon evaluation.
Room allocations, requests, and schedules are synthetic and do not reflect real-time physical sensor occupancy.
